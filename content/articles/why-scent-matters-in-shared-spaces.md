Lighting, sound and materials all shape how a space feels. Scent is the least visible of these, and often the one people remember longest.

## Consistency builds recognition

When a hotel lobby, a showroom or an office smells the same every visit, the scent becomes part of the brand. Commercial scenting systems deliver the same intensity day after day, so the experience does not depend on someone remembering to light a candle.

## Scale and control

Large rooms need controlled diffusion. Scent machines can be scheduled and adjusted so a room is scented when people are there, and not when it is empty.

## Events

For weddings, launches and activations, scent can mark the moment. It can welcome guests at the entrance or signal a change between parts of the evening.
