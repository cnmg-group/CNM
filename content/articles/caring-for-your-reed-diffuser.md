A reed diffuser is the quietest way to scent a room, and it needs very little attention. A few habits keep it working well.

## Placement

Place the diffuser where air moves gently, such as a hallway table or a shelf near a doorway. Avoid direct sunlight and heat sources, which can make the oil evaporate faster. Keep it on a stable, protected surface.

## Turning the reeds

Turning the reeds refreshes the scent. Do it over a sink, one end at a time, and wipe away any drips. Turning them too often uses the oil up faster.

## Refilling

When the scent fades, check the oil level before replacing the reeds. Reeds become saturated over time, so replace them when you refill.

## Safety

Keep diffusers away from children and pets, and follow the directions on the product label.
